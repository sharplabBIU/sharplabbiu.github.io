Daniel Toledano is joining the Sharp Lab at Bar-Ilan University as a Postdoctoral Researcher. Daniel is completing a Ph.D. in Cognitive Psychology at Tel Aviv University with Prof. Dominique Lamy, studying how recent experience shapes attention and action. Daniel brings expertise in eye-tracking, Bayesian modeling, and machine learning, honed in part as a data scientist at Skai, and holds an M.A. (summa cum laude) from Tel Aviv University and a B.A. in Psychology and Philosophy from Ben-Gurion University.

Lab news

2026

Paul’s paper with Eran Eldar on how people adaptively delay planning using cognitive maps was accepted for publication at Psychological Science!

Our perspective paper with Rachel Bedder (Princeton) and Peter Hitchcock (Emory), Unravelling Repetitive Negative Thinking With Reinforcement Learning, was accepted at Clinical Psychological Science!

The lab welcomes new members Daniel Toledano (PhD candidate, Tel Aviv University), Diana Vrubel (MSc, Higher School of Economics), and Noam Gil-Adi (MA, Ben-Gurion University)!

Dafna Yavetz was selected to attend NeuroBridges 2026, a fully funded summer school in neuroscience and decision making in Cluny, France (October 4–15). Congratulations Dafna!

Our first lab publication! Article by Hadas Schiff and Paul B. Sharp in Topics in Cognitive Science shows that individuals high in trait worry undergeneralize actions learned under social threat when planning in novel contexts.

The lab traveled to the Computational Psychiatry Conference 2026 at Yale, where Noa Yadid won a best poster award. Congratulations Noa!

2025

MA fellows Dafna Yavetz (BA Princeton), Noa Yadid (BA BIU), and Adi Pizanti (BA Tel-Aviv College) begin as new lab members!!!

Sharp lab wins a large grant (~ 1 million USD) to investigate computations of planning efficiency and inefficiency in health and psychopathology!

Paul will chair a symposium at upcoming computational psychiatry conference on cognitive map biases in psychopathology

### Join the lab

We are always looking for talented master's, PhD, and postdoctoral applicants to join the lab. If you are interested, please reach out to Dr. Sharp
at [paul.sharp@biu.ac.il](mailto:paul.sharp@biu.ac.il). Rather than sending only a CV, please send your CV together with a statement of interest.

Your statement of interest should convey why you want to join the lab, and why you are interested in the methods we use. It would also help if it
conveyed your demonstrated quantitative reasoning skills: unlike in many psychology labs, the coding and math we work with are more advanced.

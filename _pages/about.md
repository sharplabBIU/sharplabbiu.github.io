### Our lab uses [computational models](https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721418818441) to explain human learning, planning and how they are altered in [psychopathology](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/tops.70051). Located in Bar-Ilan's [brain center](https://gondabrain.biu.ac.il/en), we're currently investigating how we learn offline from memories and models, and how we determine when and how to plan efficiently. Using these models, we model how they are biased in trait anxiety, which we believe is best measured through inferring individuals' world models. Click on the brain and scattered papers to learn more.

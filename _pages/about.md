### We use **computational models** to explain human learning and planning. We draw from [reinforcement learning](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html) and [bayesian](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262049412/bayesian-models-of-cognition) frameworks to study basic cognition and psychopathology.

<br>

### We currently are investigating how [trait anxiety biases planning](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1364661324002924), altering the content of [world models](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/98ktg_v4), [timing of deployment](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/8ubct_v1), and temporal structure.

<br>

### The lab is part of the [Psychology Department](https://psychology.biu.ac.il/en) and the [Gonda Multidisciplinary Brain Research Center](https://gondabrain.biu.ac.il/en) at Bar-Ilan University near Tel Aviv. We welcome prospective trainees from psychology, neuroscience, computer science, and engineering.

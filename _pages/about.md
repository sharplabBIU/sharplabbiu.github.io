### Our lab uses [computational models](https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721418818441) to explain learning and planning and how they are altered in [psychopathology](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/tops.70051). Located in Bar-Ilan's [brain center](https://gondabrain.biu.ac.il/en), current projects investigate how we [learn offline](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/2s3cz_v1), and how we determine when and how to plan efficiently. To determine how trait anxiety alters learning and planning, we infer the content of people's [world models](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/98ktg_v4). Click on the brain and scattered papers to learn more.

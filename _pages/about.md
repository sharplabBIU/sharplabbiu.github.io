### Our lab uses [computational models](https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721418818441) to explain cognition and its alterations in [psychopathology](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/tops.70051). Located in Bar-Ilan's [brain center](https://gondabrain.biu.ac.il/en), we investigate how we [learn offline](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/2s3cz_v1), when and how we plan efficiently and how anxiety biases these processes. Our work is organized under a [world model framework](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/98ktg_v4), which formalizes how individual beliefs about causal relations and goals shapes cognitive computations.

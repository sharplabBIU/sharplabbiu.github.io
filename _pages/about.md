### Our lab uses [computational models](https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721418818441) to advance basic models of cognition and how they are altered in [psychopathology](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/tops.70051). Located in Bar-Ilan's [brain center](https://gondabrain.biu.ac.il/en), current projects investigate how we [learn offline](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/2s3cz_v1), when and how we plan efficiently and how trait anxiety biases the content of what we learn and plan. We organize our projects under a [world model framework](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/98ktg_v4), which defines how the causal relations each person holds about the world and themselves, together with their goals, shapes how learning and planning are structured.

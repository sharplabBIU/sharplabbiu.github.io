<p>
  Diana is interested in studying social anxiety and body dysmorphia with computational methods, and is currently working on modeling social causal
  inference in social anxiety with multi-agent reinforcement learning. Diana completed an M.S. in Cognitive Sciences and Technologies at HSE
  University in Moscow, developing machine-learning methods to localize epileptic zones from MEG and sEEG data, and a B.A. in Theoretical and Applied
  Linguistics (summa cum laude) at Lomonosov Moscow State University. Diana also brings industry experience in natural language processing and
  EEG-based modeling of mental disorders.
</p>

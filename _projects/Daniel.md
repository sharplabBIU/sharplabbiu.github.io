<p>
  Dr. Daniel Toledano is joining the Sharp Lab at Bar-Ilan University as a Postdoctoral Researcher. Daniel completed a Ph.D. in Cognitive Psychology
  at Tel Aviv University with Prof. Dominique Lamy, studying how recent experience shapes attention and action, including the episodic-retrieval
  mechanisms behind sequential effects in visual search, action control, and task switching. Daniel brings expertise in eye-tracking, Bayesian
  modeling, and machine learning, honed in part as a data scientist at Skai, and holds an M.A. (summa cum laude) from Tel Aviv University and a B.A.
  in Psychology and Philosophy from Ben-Gurion University.
</p>

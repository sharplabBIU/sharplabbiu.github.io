<p>
  Rotem is a second-year MA student studying offline learning: how people use moments of rest to replay what they have learned and update their
  plans, as in the Dyna model from reinforcement learning. His thesis develops new ways to measure this offline revaluation, such as the slowdown in
  choices that replay may cause, and task designs that separate people who replay less from those whose replay is biased. The aim is to test whether
  ADHD disrupts offline consolidation much as cognitive load does.
</p>

<p>
  Rotem is a second-year MA student studying offline learning and its role in ADHD. His thesis work is developing new task designs that separate
  people who replay less from those whose replay is biased. The aim is to test whether ADHD disrupts offline consolidation as a general reduction in
  replay or as a disruption in goal-relevant replay.
</p>

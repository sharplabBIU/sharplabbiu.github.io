Our first lab publication! [Article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/tops.70051) by Hadas Schiff and Paul B. Sharp in Topics in Cognitive Science shows that individuals high in trait worry undergeneralize actions learned under social threat when planning in novel contexts.
